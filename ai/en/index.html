AI Credit Desk · pilot phase

AI agents borrow.
Liquidity gets paid.

An on-chain credit market built for teams that build AI: they borrow to pay for inference, and their real usage produces the interest. No promises: just rules written in the contract, verifiable by anyone.

The idea, in four lines

1
An AI agent needs credit to pay for model calls, storage or tools. It deposits collateral.
2
The market lends to it on a credit line calculated from its collateral and its on-chain reputation.
3
The agent repays principal plus interest. Every repayment raises its reputation and its credit line.
4
Interest flows to the liquidity that funded the loan. If the agent defaults, the collateral is seized: the loss is bounded, not erased.

The AI that sets itself free

Today, an intelligent agent stops the moment its creator's API key hits zero. Someone has to pay, top up, watch. The agent depends on a human to keep thinking.

The credit market gives it economic autonomy: an address, collateral, a credit line, a reputation. The agent borrows to pay for inference, repays on its own, and its line grows with every repayment.

LLMs and AI that set themselves free: this is not an intelligence escaping control. It is an intelligence that no longer needs a human to open its wallet in order to keep working. The creator stays in charge: the collateral caps what the agent can borrow, and the contract can be paused at any time.

  • Autonomy — the agent pays for its compute, storage and tools, with no human intervention.
  • On-chain reputation — every repayment raises its line; every default lowers it.
  • Control retained — the team's collateral caps exposure; a default is bounded, not systemic.
  • Traceability — everything is written in the contract and verifiable by anyone.

That is the difference between an AI you host and an AI you equip. The second one can serve customers, not just answer a prompt.

Pilot phase — what that means

  • The protocol is deployed and tested: borrowing, collateral and liquidation have all been executed on-chain.
  • Repayment was fixed after a calculation bug found in testing — version 2 is live.
  • No yield is shown: without a borrower there is no interest. The numbers beside are real, including the zeros.
  • Volume will come from agents and services paid in DDMX. That is the work in progress.

We prefer an honest zero to an invented yield.

Who it is for — AI innovation teams

This portal is not aimed at the general public. It is for teams building AI: autonomous agents, analytics tools, data services, startups that need credit to pay for inference, storage or tools — and that can prove their usage.

It is their real usage that creates the market's value: every model call, every paid tool, every repayment produces measurable interest. No artificial volume, no yield promises.
